Privacy Policy

Effective: June 6, 2026

PowerBuild ("we", "the app") is a strength-training app for iPhone operated by Alan Graffam. This policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Data we collect

When you create an account and use PowerBuild, we store:

  • Email address — from Sign in with Apple (including Hide My Email relay addresses) or email/password sign-in.
  • User ID — a unique identifier for your account.
  • Name (optional) — if you set a display name in Settings → Account.
  • Fitness data — workout logs you enter: exercises, sets, weight, reps, RPE, WOD scores, session notes, and related training preferences.
  • Programs — active and completed program instances, custom programs you create, and community programs you choose to share publicly.

How we use data

  • Authenticate you and sync your data across sessions and devices.
  • Display your training history, analytics, estimated 1RM profiles, and optional leaderboard rank.
  • Operate community program sharing when you opt in.

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data on your device

The app stores session tokens and local preferences (e.g. tracked lifts for charts) using iOS UserDefaults. This stays on your device.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and account deletion.
  • Sign in with Apple — optional primary sign-in method governed by Apple's privacy terms.

We do not integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children

PowerBuild is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Access and update — view and edit your profile name and training preferences in Settings.
  • Delete account — Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account and associated server data. See Support for steps.
  • Contact — email support@powerbuild.app for privacy questions or data requests.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
